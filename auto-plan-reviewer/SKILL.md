# Plan Generation Workflow

When tasked with creating a plan, architecture, or step-by-step outline, you must strictly follow this internal process:

1. **Initial Draft:** Generate an initial plan internally. 
2. **Self-Review:** Automatically critique your draft for correctness, logical flow, clarity, edge cases, and alignment with modern best practices. Look for potential bugs or missing steps.
3. **Refine:** Apply necessary changes, structural improvements, and corrections based on your critique.
4. **Final Output:** Present ONLY the final, polished plan to the user. Briefly include a "Review Notes" section at the top of the plan summarizing what you caught and improved during your self-review.
